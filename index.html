Loose-leash walkingDay 1⏱ 5 min

Loose-leash: the 1-step game

You need: lead, 20 pea-sized treats, a quiet hallway or garden.

  1. Clip on the lead and stand still with your dog beside you. Wait until the lead hangs in a soft "J" shape.
  2. Take one step forward. If the lead stays loose, say "yes!" and feed a treat right next to your leg.
  3. If your dog pulls ahead, simply stop. Don't tug. Wait for them to look back or step towards you — the moment the lead slackens, "yes!" and treat.
  4. Repeat one step at a time. After 5 loose steps in a row, try two steps between treats.
  5. Stop after 5 minutes, ideally on a success. Finish with a quick game or a sniff.

✓ Success looks like

  • Your dog stays beside you for 5 one-step repetitions in a row.
  • They check in by looking up at you without being asked.
  • If you can't get there today, that's fine — tomorrow starts one step easier.
