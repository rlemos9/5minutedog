Privacy, in plain words

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who runs this

5 Minute Dog is run by Rui Pereira. Contact: rlemospereira9@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the signup form: your email address, your WhatsApp number (optional), your delivery preference, your dog's name, age and biggest problem, and whether you ticked the box to get the daily exercises.

Why

To let you know when 5 Minute Dog launches and, if you ticked the box, to send you a daily 5-minute training exercise tailored to your dog. That's it.

Where it's stored

Your details are stored in a private Google Sheet (Google Workspace / Google Drive), and emails are sent through Gmail. Your browser also keeps a copy of your submission on your own device.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or 12 months after the last email we sent you — whichever comes first.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete your details, reply to any of our emails or write to rlemospereira9@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't share them with anyone for marketing.
